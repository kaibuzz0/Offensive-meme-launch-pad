MEME CITY / DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

FROM TOKEN ICONS
TO LIVING STREETS.

Six existing beta mints are the first character identities. We are building an original browser world in stages. The game has no new blockchain contracts, promised earnings, or paid founder access. Optional purchases of existing beta tokens open the wallet flow and do not currently grant verified game perks.

NEW / PLAY TOGETHER
ALPHA 09

Bring your crew.

Shared rooms support up to eight guests: quick play, friend codes, invite links, synchronized city movement, shared interiors, bot combat, room chat and play-credit trading. The server validates movement and rewards. Temporary reconnects keep the same guest session; online room progress resets when the service restarts. Your solo fortress and squad stay separate.

Public guest rooms are hosted on Render and connected to this site. Open Together for quick play or a friend-code room. The free service may need about a minute to wake up after being idle. Browser hosting, permanent online accounts and blockchain rewards are not active.

NEW / OPEN DOORS
ALPHA 08

Step inside your city.

Explore furnished interiors in all eleven city buildings, plus your built Meme HQ. Walk up to a glowing entrance and press E. Visit the arcade, gallery, print studio, club, cafe, garage, shops and more. Find room mementos and use activity terminals. Challenges and combat take place outside.

Jointed walk cycles follow actual travel, with foot lift, arm swing and body lean. Trees, street furniture, pedestrians and traffic now share depth sorting with characters and buildings. Your discoveries join your existing saved progress.

NEW / CITY RISING
ALPHA 07

A reason to make this city yours.

Follow an eight-chapter city journey and discover twelve landmark diamonds across all five neighborhoods. Claim chapter rewards in Journey; discoveries award play credits and XP once each. Five scripted NPC guides introduce the journey, street medicine, hoverboard travel, fortress command and arena survival.

Ride a free city-loaner hoverboard with acceleration and collision. Use V or Ride to mount; firing and dashing put you back on foot. Carry medkits (H / Heal), each restoring 45 HP, and restock near Patch for 25 play credits. Build a Meme HQ and upgrade it through three levels: Outpost, Stronghold and Citadel. Each level adds 20 defender max HP and strengthens your out-of-combat recovery zone.

The Arena now has a three-wave survival run: melee raiders, ranged enemies and the Rug Baron with 420 HP. Start beside Marshal, bring following defenders and clear all three waves within four minutes for 180 play credits and 160 XP. A run ends when you leave, get busted, abandon it or run out of time. There are no real-money arena rewards.

City Rising adds an in-game clock, dusk-to-night lighting, light rain, landmark effects, guide characters, a boss health bar, hit glows and hoverboard trails. Existing solo saves migrate into the new journey; all equipment, fortress construction and hired defenders carry over. Active arena runs and riding reset between visits.

01 / LIVE ALPHA 07

The first neighborhood

Explore a neon city with ambient traffic and pedestrians. Fight hostile bots, evade police patrols, dash, collect health and credits, and run repeatable combat, delivery, and pursuit contracts. Choose a beta-token avatar, upgrade gear, and save solo progress on this browser. Night Shift adds attack warnings, cover-based cop searches, obstacle navigation, stationary dashes, a mission countdown and results, a phone map, pause/fullscreen controls, and a simpler toolbar. Held fire is simulated independently of rendering speed. Streetwear adds human character models, meme-print shirts and jackets, ticker stripes, repeating trouser graphics, coin-name panels, appearance options, and a full-body wardrobe.

NEW / LIVE SOLO

Four corners. Your own fortress.

The expanded 6,720 × 3,320 map connects Founder Square, Fortress Heights, Creator Quarter, Arena District and Social Boardwalk with walkable roads and garden paths. Map buttons mark directions; every district is part of the same world. Build a personal 16 × 10 fortress plot with concrete walls, towers, a Meme HQ, banners, trees, lamps and stone paving. Preview, rotate, remove for a full play-credit refund and walk through your own build. The optional starter layout costs 157 play credits. Your plot saves with your solo character in this browser.

The Creator Quarter has six existing beta clothing collections. Street Sweep starts locally in the Arena; delivery and pursuit jobs start in Founder Square. The Social Boardwalk has a waterfront, NPC pedestrians and local emotes. Hire up to four defenders with play credits through Squad: Block Sentry (20), Street Ranger (55) and Heavy Defender (90). Give Hold here, Patrol fortress and Follow me orders. Fight a four-raider defense drill with your squad for 60 play credits and 40 XP; downed defenders recover after 15 seconds. Player walking, two-handed rifle aiming, recoil and close-range projectile behavior have been revised. Shared fortress visits, linked player plots, creator uploads, co-op events and online meetups remain planned. District building is currently solo only, including when the optional room server is running.

Architecture, plaza materials, landscaping, traffic art, shadows and lighting have been revised. This is still a stylized 2D canvas game; a full 3D renderer and custom 3D characters would be a separate engine milestone.

02 / ONLINE GUEST ALPHA

Meet in a shared city

A room server is included in the repository: up to eight rooms of eight temporary guest sessions, shared movement, chat, authoritative mission rewards and inventory, and player listings traded for play credits. This GitHub Pages deployment connects to the public HTTPS room service through Together.

Before a larger release: add durable player accounts, moderation, stronger abuse controls, operational monitoring, and recovery. Temporary room state currently resets on server restart.

03 / PLANNED

Wallet identity & holding tiers

Connect a wallet, prove control with a scoped sign-in message, and verify balances server-side against the six allowlisted Solana mints. Publish each token’s supply-normalized tier rules, a maximum starting boost, snapshot timing, and what happens after selling.

Separate earned XP from holding perks. Never trust a balance supplied by a browser. No holder tier or token balance is verified in the current game.

04 / PLANNED

A marketplace worth using

Turn temporary room trading into durable item ownership with transaction history, atomic escrow, reconnect recovery, fraud checks, and support tools. Keep play credits separate from real assets.

Any future SOL or token marketplace needs a separately reviewed settlement design and explicit wallet approval. No game item is currently an NFT, redeemable asset, or source of cash rewards.

05 / PLANNED

Creators bring their worlds

Creator profiles, moderated artwork uploads, preset avatar bodies, shared emotes, and connected community spaces. Start with token artwork on a shared character model; later add reviewed custom 3D mascots and animations.

Prelaunch spaces let people discover a meme and meet its community before token creation. Launch approval and audit evidence remain separate from artwork approval.

06 / PLANNED

More city. More chaos.

Drivable vehicles, cooperative missions, events, richer animation, and additional neighborhoods. The current build has arcade combat against enemy bots and cops, health and respawning. Traffic cars remain ambient scenery. A free personal hoverboard is playable in City Rising; drivable cars are a future milestone. Player-versus-player damage remains disabled.

Expand after performance, mobile controls, server capacity, moderation, and saving are dependable. No release dates are promised.
