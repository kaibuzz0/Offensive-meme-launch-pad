CREATOR GUIDE / PROPOSAL V0.1

BRING THE MEME.
BUILD SOMETHING.

A proposed launch program for creators who show up before launch and stay involved afterward.

In development — applications are expressions of interest.

We are not accepting payments, hosting community token creation, tracking eligibility, or distributing creator pool funds yet. These draft terms need to be finalized and published before anyone pays or launches.

Before a token goes live.

01 / PROJECT PREVIEW

Give people time to discover you.

Proposed minimum: a public project preview for 7 days, with artwork, creator profile, launch plan, token allocations, and a community discussion link. Target: 50 verified expressions of interest from distinct people before scheduling.

Previews and interest verification are planned. Joining Telegram today is not a project reservation or purchase commitment.

02 / PROJECT REVIEW

Show the details.

Review the team’s wallet control, artwork rights, mint and freeze authorities, token allocation, vesting, liquidity plan, and disclosed risks. Publish findings, unresolved issues, and reviewer identity before scheduling.

03 / AUDIT STATUS

Evidence before a badge.

Use “Not reviewed,” “In review,” or “Review published.” An independent security audit requires a linked report naming the auditor, date, scope, and contract addresses. A platform review is not an audit.

Custom contracts or unresolved high-risk findings must receive independent review before launch approval. No existing beta token has an audit badge.

04 / SCHEDULE & LAUNCH

A date the community can follow.

After approval: publish the confirmed fees, full mint address, provider, launch time, and disclosures. A countdown or schedule is only confirmed after the launch integration is operational.

Fees that people can see.

Draft pricing for discussion, in SOL. No payments are enabled.

0.1 SOLApplication review proposal. Refundable before review starts; otherwise pays for review, regardless of approval.
0.5 SOLHosting / launch service proposal. Only payable after approval and a confirmed launch booking; refundable if the platform cancels before launch.
SeparateNetwork, launch-provider, liquidity, and third-party audit costs. Any extra cost must be quoted and accepted before work begins.

No proposed trading surcharge. Existing beta wallet swaps still add no OFFENSIVE fee. Paying a review fee does not buy approval or pool eligibility.

Earn a place in the creator pool.

A shared, fee-funded program for qualifying community creators. Separate from $OFFENDED’s creator-reported holder rewards. Holding the platform token is not a requirement.

What funds the pool?

Draft split: 20% of actually collected hosting / launch service fees goes to the creator pool; 80% supports platform operations. Review fees, token purchase funds, liquidity deposits, and third-party pass-through costs are excluded. Refunds reduce eligible fee revenue before allocation. No revenue means no new pool funding.

What would a coin need to qualify?
  • An approved community launch, with at least 7 complete days live and a published review with no unresolved high-risk findings.
  • At least 100 distinct, non-team holders at the weekly snapshot and 20 SOL of eligible trading volume across the previous 7 days, active on at least 4 of those days.
  • At least 0.1 SOL in cumulative, settled eligible platform service fees attributable to that project, after refunds. This is a one-time revenue qualification; ongoing participation still requires passing the weekly activity checks.
  • A public creator update that week, disclosed team allocations, and no identified wash trading, linked-wallet farming, undisclosed supply changes, or unresolved liquidity-plan breaches.
  • Beta / founder test tokens are excluded from this proposed community pool.

These are starting thresholds, not live measurements. Holder wallets are not proof of distinct people. Related wallets, self-trades, airdrop dust, bots, and coordinated circular trades must be excluded through documented review. No price-rise or market-cap target is required.

How would shares be calculated?

Weekly UTC accounting: a qualifying project’s weight is its screened, eligible trading volume in SOL for that week divided by the total screened eligible volume of all qualifying projects that week. Its proposed payout is that weight multiplied by the available pool, capped at 25% of the available pool per project.

Example: with a 10 SOL pool and a project representing 10% of qualifying volume, its share is 1 SOL. A project representing 60% would receive the 2.5 SOL cap. Capped excess and any unallocated balance roll into the next week. If nobody qualifies, the whole pool rolls forward.

Balances below 0.01 SOL roll forward until the threshold is reached. No payout is promised: funds, qualifying activity, verified calculations, and a functioning payout system are required.

Who checks the numbers?

Before activation, publish the pool wallet, collection addresses, supported venues, snapshot methodology, exclusions, project-by-project calculations, and payout transaction links. Proposed process: publish a weekly provisional report, allow 7 days for challenges, then settle reviewed payouts.

Flagged projects are held pending documented review and an appeal. Criteria changes apply only to future periods with at least 7 days’ notice. Identity checks and a payout system are not yet implemented; no live statistics are displayed.
